# Integration guidance

This is a project-authored integration skill, not a skill supplied by the upstream repository.

Source: https://github.com/dashersw/liquid-glass-js

The upstream is vanilla JavaScript, not an npm package. Vendor the Container source at a recorded revision with its MIT notice. Supply an explicit decorative snapshot; do not use full-document html2canvas on analyst content. Add disposal for scroll listeners and WebGL contexts. Keep text and buttons semantic and readable above the shader canvas.
